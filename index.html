Faker: The GOAT of League of Legends

Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, is widely considered the greatest League of Legends player of all time. He has spent his carrer with T1 and has won multiple World Championships while becoming one of the most influential esports player in history.

What Faker Is Great At

  • Exceptional mechanical skill and reflexes
  • Deep understanding of the game and its meta
  • Ability to adapt to different champions and playstyles
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Consistent performance under pressure

The Unkillable Demon King

Faker earned the nickname "The Unkillable Demon King" because of his incredible ability to outplay opponents and win seemingly impossible situations. The nickname became one of the most famous titles in esports and is now synonymous with his legendary career.

Recognition Beyond Esports

Faker's influence extends beyond gaming. In 2026, he was appointed as an honorary police officer by Korean National Police Agency in recognition of his positive influence and role as a model for young people.

Faker was also exempted from South Korea's Mandatory military service after winning a gold medal with Team Korea at the Asian Games, one of the most significant achievements of his career.

Global Recognition

In 2026, Faker was named to TIME's inaugural TIME100 Sports list, recognizing him as one of the most influential figures in sports. His inclusion highlighted the growing importance of esports and his role in bringing competitive gaming to a global audience.

The Cheongnyong Medal

Faker became the first esports player to receive the Cheongnong Medal, the highest class of South Korea's Order of Sport Merit. This honor recognized his contributuions to esports and his role in elevating South Korea's reputation on the international stage.

Legacy and Impact

Faker is widely regarded as the greatest League of Legends player of all time and one of the most influential figures in esports history. Through his championships, sportsmanship, leadership, and longevity, he helped establish esports as a respected global form of competition.

Millions of players around the world have been inspired by Faker's dedication, humility, and pursuit of excellence. His impact on esports continues to shape the industry and inspire future generations of players.
