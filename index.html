Faker: The GOAT of League of Legends

Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, widely considered the greatest Leage of Legends player of all time. He has spent his carrer with T1 and has won multiple World Championships while becoming one of the most influential esports player in history.

What Faker Is Great At

  • Exceptional mechanical skill and reflexes
  • Deep understanding of the game and its meta
  • Ability to adapt to different champions and playstyles
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Consistent performance under pressure
